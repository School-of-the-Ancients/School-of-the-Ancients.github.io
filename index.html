The learning experience

School of the Ancients · Professor

The first learning experience built on Matrix Loading Operator. A Professor guides the conversation and can ask Matrix to create or load an exhibit while you keep learning.

  • Talk by text or voice with historically inspired AI teachers.
  • Follow short lessons, practice an idea, and see your progress.
  • Explore freely, save your adventure, and return later.

The creation platform

Matrix Loading Operator

An AI-assisted WebXR sandbox for creating and interacting with digital worlds in a browser, AR or VR. It can be used for open-ended creation as well as teaching.

  • Request images, panoramic backgrounds, 3D objects and animations.
  • Load reusable assets or generate new exhibits with connected tools.
  • Place, interact with and save the world you build.

Put it into words

Make a prediction, explore what happens, and explain your reasoning. Guided lessons track your progress; free exploration stays available.

Professors are AI portrayals inspired by historical figures, not authentic voices or recordings. Generated explanations and exhibits can need correction.

Where the project is today

The core experience is running. The next stage is making it easier and more reliable for other people to use.

  • Matrix sandboxWorking prototypeWorld creation, asset loading, panoramas, interaction and saves are implemented. Connected tools and local setup are required.
  • Professor learningWorking prototypeAI mentors, text and voice conversation, mini-lessons, objectives and saved progress are implemented. Lesson quality and rewards are still being refined.
  • Browser & devicesIn progressDesktop browser learning and Quest AR/VR are the current focus. Phone layouts and device-specific controls need more testing; immersive modes require compatible hardware and a supported browser.
  • Hosted user accountsPlannedSimple signup, isolated user data, hosted creation jobs and multiplayer classrooms are future work. This is not yet a public, self-service hosted product.

Progress against the original roadmap

The original vision set out five milestones. Here is where they stand in the working prototype as of October 26, 2026.

  • Implemented: core VR world loader (Matrix Operator).
  • Implemented: AI historical mentor engine.
  • Implemented: Socratic dialogue with guided questions and follow-up.
  • Planned: multiplayer classrooms for collaborative debate.
  • Partly implemented: gamification. Quests, XP, levels and achievements exist; unlockable mentors remain future work.

Implemented means present in the prototype, with refinement and device testing still ongoing.
