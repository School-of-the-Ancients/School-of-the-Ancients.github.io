Curiosity is the starting point

Ask. Look closer.
Keep the conversation going.

What if you could ask about wireless electricity, then explore the coils with a professor beside you? That’s the experience we’re building.

01

Start with a question.

Talk by text or live voice with a historically inspired AI professor. Ask a follow-up, challenge an explanation, or take the conversation somewhere new.

02

Give the idea a shape.

Your professor can request a 3D exhibit, loaded from the catalog or created with connected tools. Conversation can continue while it prepares.

03

Explore it together.

Point to a part. Ask what it does. The professor can refer to the selected object and describe its parts, helping you connect the explanation to what you see.

Professors are AI portrayals inspired by historical figures, not authentic voices or recordings. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by those people, their estates, or associated institutions. AI explanations and generated exhibits can need correction. Learning outcomes have not been independently established. Exhibit preparation takes time; this is a working prototype.

The world behind the lesson

A conversation with room for discovery.

School of the Ancients is the learning experience. Our dynamic creation platform loads exhibits, places objects, and brings AI professors into browser, AR and VR worlds.

Exhibits can be reused from a catalog or generated with tools such as Blender. The same platform also supports open-ended world creation.

How this connects to the original vision

The original vision describes historical mentors, Socratic dialogue and immersive learning. In the current prototype:

  • The core world loader, historical mentor engine and guided dialogue are implemented.
  • Quests, XP, levels and achievements exist; unlockable mentors remain future work.
  • Community shares starting questions; complete prepared-scene sharing is still in development.
  • Multiplayer classrooms are planned.

Implemented means present in the prototype, with quality and device testing ongoing.
