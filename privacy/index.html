Privacy notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026

School of the Ancients LLC operates School of the Ancients and its Matrix learning prototype. For questions about personal information, access, correction or deletion, contact matt@schooloftheancients.com.

Website and waitlist

Our public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site. External links, including YouTube demo links, connect you to the named service when you follow them.

The waitlist form is provided by Tally. It loads on this website only when you choose “Load signup form”; you can also choose to open Tally directly. Tally receives connection information when its form loads. If you submit, we receive your email and your choice about early-access invitations and occasional project updates. Waitlist submissions are held in Tally and a private connected Google Sheet. Do not put sensitive information in the form.

You can withdraw from these updates by replying to an email or contacting us. We will stop future marketing to that address; we may retain the minimum suppression record needed to respect your choice. Tally’s privacy notice explains its own processing.

Optional analytics

The homepage and original-vision page offer Google Analytics only after you allow it. Analytics can process page visits, device/browser information, traffic sources and events such as opening a demo or completing the waitlist. Our event code does not send form answers or email addresses to Analytics. You can change your choice using “Analytics preferences” on those pages. Declining analytics does not prevent using the site or waitlist.

Learning prototype

The hosted service uses Supabase/Postgres for accounts and saved work, with Cloudflare providing network delivery and security. These services process information needed to operate and protect the application.

If you create an account or use a learning session, the service processes account details, questions, conversations, learning progress, saved adventures and content you choose to submit. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; do not send them in a chat or email. Signed-in shared content may be visible to the audience you choose.

AI features send relevant questions and conversation or scene context to the configured AI provider. The current hosted application uses OpenAI. Optional cloud voice sends microphone audio, and visual review can send selected images or room-camera frames. A browser permission prompt permits device access; read the feature notice before starting. You can stop capture without deleting an existing conversation.

OpenAI has its own retention and safety-processing rules. Stopping a session or deleting our copy does not automatically delete provider records. See OpenAI’s API data controls. Local development versions and earlier demos can use different providers, including Google Gemini. See the earlier-prototype notice.

Retention and requests

The prototype does not yet apply an automatic deletion schedule to every category of saved data. Waitlist records are used for the updates you requested; account records and saved work support the continuing service. To request access, correction, account closure or deletion, email us from the address associated with the data. We may need to verify the request. Do not include your password or unnecessary sensitive information.

We assess requests under applicable law, including any required retention for security, legal obligations and records of your communication preferences. Backups and provider systems may have separate deletion cycles. Moving work to Trash, ending voice capture or signing out is not the same as erasing all copies. We do not promise immediate deletion from every system.

Children and sensitive information

Waitlist signups and new accounts are intended for people age 13 and older. We use a broad age range rather than a birth date for the signup screen. The website waitlist screen uses the selection only in your browser; the account service records the declared age range and terms acceptance with the account. This is an experimental learning service. Do not submit another person’s personal information without permission, or use room capture around people who have not agreed. If you believe a child has submitted information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change. The date above identifies this version. This notice does not waive rights you have under applicable privacy law.
