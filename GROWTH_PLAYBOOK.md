# Early-access list growth — launch playbook

## Goal

Initial target: 100 people who actively opt in. No paid ads, purchased lists, scraped addresses, or unsolicited bulk mailing.

## Verify before promotion

- [ ] Submit one user-owned test address on https://schooloftheancients.com with explicit consent.
- [ ] Confirm the submission appears in the owner dashboard for Formspree form `xvkkkbrq`. Remove test afterward.
- [ ] Record the current real subscriber count. Website source alone cannot tell us the count.
- [ ] Test mobile and desktop form validation, delivery and opt-out process.
- [ ] Check how Formspree handles notifications and spam.

## Simple message

> What if you could ask an AI historical professor a question and explore the answer in your room?
>
> School of the Ancients is an experimental browser/AR/VR learning experience with conversational AI mentors and interactive 3D exhibits. Watch the demo and join the early-access list: https://schooloftheancients.com/?utm_source=community&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=oct2026_launch

## Zero-cost distribution

1. Post a 30–45 second authentic demonstration to a personal LinkedIn or social account, and link to the waitlist.
2. Share a classroom-relevant example in communities that explicitly allow project showcases; follow each community's rules and avoid repeated promotional posts.
3. Ask interested CU Boulder peers, XR developers, educators and hackathon contacts for feedback on a lesson; invite them to subscribe voluntarily.
4. Add a waitlist call-to-action to the existing YouTube demo description and future Devpost submissions, where permitted.
5. Run one small controlled campaign at a time: track `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` fields in Formspree submissions.

## Measure

Use Formspree confirmed new subscribers (not form impressions or page visits), tagged source, and unsubscribe requests. Do not claim conversion rates without visitor counts. Keep messages occasional and honor opt-outs promptly.

## Customer discovery

Invite subscribers to answer a short voluntary question when appropriate: learner, parent, educator, creator, or XR developer? Segment only with explicit permission. Do not promise launch dates or available features beyond the working prototype.
