Original vision

This page summarizes the ideas behind the October 2025 project. The public wording was updated on November 6, 2026 to clarify the intended experience and its limitations.

See current development status.

The idea

School of the Ancients began with a question. Could conversation, guided questioning and explorable 3D worlds make it easier to engage with an unfamiliar idea?

The vision brings together three areas of development.

  • AI-assisted lesson planning that adapts to a learner’s questions.
  • Fictionalized educational mentors inspired by historical people and their work.
  • A dynamic creation platform that can load and arrange exhibits and environments.

Learning through exploration

The aim is to pair discussion with things a learner can examine. Guided questions, examples and reflection are design goals. Learning outcomes have not been independently established.

Mentor dialogue, voices and appearances are AI-generated interpretations. Historical accuracy varies. The project has no affiliation with or endorsement from the depicted people, their estates or associated institutions.

Current development

The project remains a prototype. Feature availability and device support are being tested. Multiplayer classrooms and broader hosted access are development goals.
